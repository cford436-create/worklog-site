Privacy Policy

Privacy questions

This Privacy Policy describes how the WorkLog iOS application and this public website handle information. WorkLog is designed to keep work records on the device where the app is installed.

Information WorkLog Stores

WorkLog stores the following information in the app's local storage on your device:

  • shift start and end times, including shift-start correction details;
  • activities, timers, durations, custom activity definitions, and activity settings;
  • work notes and their timestamps;
  • generated and saved end-of-shift reports;
  • app settings and onboarding status; and
  • identifiers used locally to avoid processing the same Siri or App Intent request more than once.

How Information Is Used

WorkLog uses locally stored information to display current and previous shifts, calculate work and downtime totals, manage activities and notes, generate reports, remember settings, and process supported Siri actions.

The current WorkLog implementation does not include application analytics, advertising, a WorkLog remote API, or a third-party crash-reporting SDK.

Accounts and Cloud Storage

WorkLog does not require a WorkLog account. It does not operate a WorkLog backend or cloud-sync service, and it does not keep a server copy of your work records. Device-level backup and restoration are controlled by you, Apple, and your device settings.

Data Sharing

WorkLog does not include a WorkLog service that uploads or shares your shifts, activities, notes, reports, or settings. Information may leave the app when you deliberately copy a report and then paste or share it elsewhere.

Apple, the operating system, and platform or hosting providers may process technical or service information independently under their own terms and privacy policies.

Siri and Apple Services

When you use Siri or App Shortcuts with WorkLog, Apple system services handle features such as speech recognition, dictation, shortcut discovery, and system security decisions. WorkLog receives the resulting intent, supplied parameters, an invocation identifier, and a timestamp so it can create or update a local record and avoid duplicate processing.

WorkLog's Siri confirmations are brief and do not read back historical notes, previous reports, or past shift details. Apple's processing of Siri requests is outside WorkLog's local persistence layer.

Clipboard

WorkLog places report text on the system clipboard only when you choose Copy. Other apps or system features may be able to read clipboard content according to your device settings and operating-system behavior.

Device Permissions

The current WorkLog application does not implement access to location, contacts, the camera, the photo library, the microphone, or push notifications. Siri and dictation are Apple system features and remain subject to your iPhone settings and Apple's controls.

Data Retention

WorkLog records remain in the app's local storage until you edit or delete them, remove the app, or the device or operating system otherwise removes or restores app data. Deleting or reinstalling WorkLog may permanently remove locally stored records. WorkLog cannot recover a separate server copy.

Children's Privacy

WorkLog is a general-purpose work-record tool and is not designed specifically for children. Parents and guardians should decide whether the app is appropriate for a child in their care. WorkLog does not create user accounts or collect information through a WorkLog-operated online service.

Security

WorkLog relies on iOS and your device's security controls to protect locally stored app data. You are responsible for securing your device and deciding where copied report text is pasted or shared. No storage or security method can be guaranteed to prevent every risk.

Third-Party Services

WorkLog uses Apple platform services for app distribution and supported Siri/App Intent features. Those services are governed by Apple's policies. The public WorkLog website contains no account system, analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, or site-set cookies. Its hosting provider may process routine request information needed to deliver the site under the provider's own policy.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This policy may be updated if WorkLog's features or information practices change. The current version will be posted on this page with its effective date. Review the policy when installing an update that changes WorkLog's data practices.

Contact

For privacy questions or WorkLog support, email worklogappsupport@gmail.com.
