01

Getting Started

Open WorkLog Voice and complete the short introduction. On Today, start a shift when work begins. Add timed or fixed-duration activities and notes as the day progresses, then end the shift to create your report.

02

Starting and Ending a Shift

Use Start Shift and End Shift on Today or through a supported Siri action. If a shift was started late, edit its start time and optionally record the reason. The corrected time is used throughout WorkLog Voice without creating a duplicate shift.

Completed shifts and their saved details are available in History.

03

Custom Activities

Open Settings → Manage Activities to add or edit activity names, icons, logging modes, default durations, and accounting behavior. New custom activities can be configured for the way you work.

04

Downtime

A completed activity contributes to Total Downtime when Counts toward downtime is enabled for that activity. The same total appears on Today, in History, and in generated reports.

05

Clocked-Out Time

Clocked-out time is separate from downtime. Activities marked Clocked out during this activity contribute to the clocked-out total and are reflected consistently in the shift summary and report.

06

End-of-Shift Reports

Ending a shift generates a chronological report with shift duration, downtime, clocked-out time, activities, and notes. You can edit and save the report. Choose Copy Report only when you want the report text placed on the system clipboard.

07

Siri Hands-Free Logging

WorkLog Voice includes supported Siri and App Shortcut actions for starting and ending shifts, logging common activities, ending the current activity, and adding a work note. Try saying “Start my shift in WorkLog Voice” or “Log a break in WorkLog Voice.”

Some actions may ask you to choose an activity or dictate note text. iOS controls shortcut availability and may request authentication.

08

Locked-Screen Siri Troubleshooting

  1. Confirm Siri is enabled, including Allow Siri When Locked, in iPhone Settings.
  2. Open WorkLog Voice once after installing a new build so shortcuts can register.
  3. Say the full phrase, including “in WorkLog Voice.”
  4. Try the same command while unlocked to distinguish registration from a lock-screen restriction.
  5. After restarting the iPhone, unlock it once before testing access to protected app data.

iOS makes the final security decision and may require authentication even when a WorkLog Voice intent supports locked-device use.

09

Local Data and Reinstall Warning

WorkLog Voice records are stored locally on your device. There is no WorkLog Voice account, backend, or cloud-sync service. Deleting or reinstalling the app may permanently remove shifts, activities, notes, reports, and settings. WorkLog Voice cannot restore a server copy.

10

Version and Build

Open Settings in WorkLog Voice. The installed version and native build number appear near the bottom of the screen and on About WorkLog Voice. Include both values when requesting help.

11

Contact Support

Email worklogappsupport@gmail.com with your WorkLog Voice version and build number, iPhone model, iOS version, and the steps that led to the issue. Avoid including confidential work-note or report content.

For information about local storage and Apple services, read the WorkLog Voice Privacy Policy.
